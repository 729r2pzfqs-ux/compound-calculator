Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

CompoundCalc.ai ("we", "us") operates the website https://compoundcalc.ai. This page explains what information is collected when you use the site and how it is used.

Calculations Stay on Your Device

All calculator inputs (investment amounts, interest rates, time periods) are processed entirely in your browser. They are never sent to, or stored on, our servers.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site (pages visited, approximate location, device type). Google Analytics may set cookies and collect your IP address and usage data. This information is aggregated and does not personally identify you. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on or by blocking cookies in your browser settings.

Third-Party Services

The site loads resources from third-party CDNs (Tailwind CSS, Chart.js, Google Fonts). These providers may log standard request data such as your IP address as part of serving those files. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may collect visitor IP addresses for security purposes; see GitHub's privacy statement.

What We Don't Do

  • No user accounts and no registration.
  • No sale, rental, or sharing of personal data.
  • No advertising networks or tracking pixels beyond Google Analytics.
  • No storage of your financial inputs.

Children's Privacy

This site does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
